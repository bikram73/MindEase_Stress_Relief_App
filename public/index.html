About MindEase

MindEase was created with simplicity and user privacy in mind. We believe that mental wellness tools should be accessible without complexity, subscriptions, or intrusive data collection. Our app focuses on providing instant stress-relief through a clean, calming interface, utilizing only local storage for your data.

Our mission is to empower individuals to take control of their emotional well-being by offering effective, easy-to-use tools that fit seamlessly into their daily lives.
