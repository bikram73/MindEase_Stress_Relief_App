Tips & Resources

Simple, science-backed ways to calm your mind and body.

Dashboard

4-7-8 Breathing

Inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 7, and exhale for 8. This lowers heart rate and anxiety.

Body Scan Meditation

Lie down, close your eyes, and release tension from each muscle group.

Guided Visualization

Picture a peaceful place and focus on sensory details to reduce stress.

Gratitude Reflection

Write three things you are grateful for to improve mood and resilience.

Mindful Walking

Notice each step and your surroundings during a short walk to reset focus.

Progressive Muscle Relaxation

Gently tense and release muscle groups from toes to head.
